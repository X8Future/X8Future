## Welcome to the Crew

Future Crew is a community built around driving. Everything you need is in one place: a huge custom car lineup, a competitive leaderboard, a ranking system, custom game modes, and a launcher that gets you on the road in minutes.

## 🚗 40+ Custom Cars

A massive garage of custom-built cars made for Future Crew, with new additions all the time. 

[Browse the garage →](https://cdn.futurecrew.online)

## 🏆 Competitive Leaderboard

Every driver is ranked. Climb the live leaderboard, see where you stand against the rest of the crew, and compete for the top spot. Check it out inside our Discord or launcher!

## 🎖️ Rank System

Earn your place in the community by progressing through the ranks:

With 16 different ranks to earn, you will never be bored; climb the leaderboard and earn higher ranks

## 🔧 What We're Working On

There's a lot happening behind the scenes at Future Crew, and we're always building. Here's a look at what's in the works:

- Launcher updates: a smoother, faster, more polished launcher with new features, a refreshed look, and better setup for new drivers.
- New cars: more custom builds joining the garage, each made to stand out on our servers.
- Custom scripts and features: new in-game systems and tools that make driving on Future Crew feel different from any other server.
- Leaderboard and rank upgrades: better tracking, clearer progression, and more ways to show off your standing.
- Game modes and events: new ways to play and compete, from community events to custom modes like Cops & Robbers.
- Faster downloads: ongoing improvements to how content is delivered so you spend less time waiting and more time driving.

Have an idea or something you'd like to see? Drop it in the Discord. Community feedback shapes a lot of what we build.
